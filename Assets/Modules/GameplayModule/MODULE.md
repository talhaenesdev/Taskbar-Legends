# GameplayModule

## Purpose
The idle battle: a party of heroes fights down an endless dungeon on its own, floor by floor, earning EXP and gold, levelling up, picking an upgrade or a new hero at every level, and climbing the ranks from E to S.

## Decisions
- A party of three (CD_Gameplay.Party) shares level, EXP, gold and rank; each member has its own HP. One member acts per turn in order, the creature strikes every `EnemyAttackEveryTurns` turns at the front member still standing, and a fallen member is revived when that creature dies.
- The battle loop is paced by itself: `Tick`'s first step waits `CD_Gameplay.TurnSeconds`, its last dispatches the next `Tick`.
- A defeat on either side runs its own flow (`EnemyDefeated`, `HeroDefeated`) inside the turn, so the turn after it sees the new creature.
- Progress (level, exp, gold, kills) is saved as JSON in PlayerPrefs after every kill and every death. The project has no Local Save module to file an `SD_` asset with.

- Every level gained earns one level-up choice (`PendingUpgrades`, saved). One offer is open at a time: a hero the level has unlocked is always a card until it joins, the rest are upgrades drawn by weight. The earned choice is spent when a card is applied, so a game closed with an offer up offers it again.
- An offer is waited out on its own signal (`WatchUpgradeOffer`) and its first card is taken for the player after `AutoPickSeconds` - an idle game cannot wait on a player who is not looking - and the battle never waits on it.
- Floors: `FloorKillsToBoss` kills, then the floor's boss (the strongest creature the floor has unlocked). A boss kill descends a floor; a fallen party starts its floor's kills over. Creature level climbs with the floor, not with the party's level.
- Time away: the save carries `LastSavedTicks`; at the start of a run the time since is rewarded as `OfflineKillsPerMinute` x `OfflineEfficiency` kills of an average creature of the saved floor, capped at `OfflineMaxHours`. It is granted after the run is announced, so the window is up to show it.
- Shop items (`CD_Gameplay.Shop`) use the same `UpgradeKind`s as level-up stacks and are summed into the same stats, always for the whole party. A purchase names an item by its index in the config's list; the save attaches by `Id`.
- A boss kill raises its creature as a shadow soldier with `ShadowChance`, up to `MaxShadows`; each adds `ShadowAttackBonus` to party attack.
- Reawakening needs `ReawakenMinFloor`; it pays `Floor x EssencePerFloor` Essence and resets level, EXP, gold, floor, recruits, pending choices and level-up stacks. The shop, the shadow army, Essence, kills and best floor are kept. Each Essence adds `EssenceBonus` to attack, HP, gold and EXP. An open level-up choice is withdrawn with it.
- A new creature's walk-in is set in seconds (`EnemyArrivalSeconds`) and turned into turns at spawn, so Speed upgrades never let the party strike a creature the screen still shows walking in.

## Known gaps
- No skills or gear yet; gold is spent only in the shop.
- Offline time is rewarded at the floor saved, so a party that would have died there still earns its kills.
- The auto-pick always takes the first card, so an absent player gets the recruit first and then whatever upgrade happened to be drawn first.
