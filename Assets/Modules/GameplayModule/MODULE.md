# GameplayModule

## Purpose
The idle battle: the hero fights an endless line of creatures on its own, earning EXP and gold, levelling up and climbing the ranks from E to S.

## Concepts
gameplay, run, idle, auto battle, combat turn, tick, hero, enemy, creature, spawn, exp, gold, level up, rank, E-S rank, revive, save, CD_Gameplay, IHeroModel, ICombatModel

## Decisions
- The battle loop is paced by itself: `Tick`'s first step waits `CD_Gameplay.TurnSeconds`, its last dispatches the next `Tick`.
- A defeat on either side runs its own flow (`EnemyDefeated`, `HeroDefeated`) inside the turn, so the turn after it sees the new creature.
- Progress (level, exp, gold, kills) is saved as JSON in PlayerPrefs after every kill and every death. The project has no Local Save module to file an `SD_` asset with.

## Known gaps
- No upgrades, skills or gear to spend gold on yet; gold only accumulates.

Update: never
