# GameplayScreenModule

## Purpose
The System window the idle battle is shown in: hero level, rank, HP and EXP bars, stats, the arena with damage numbers, the battle log and System notices. Dragging it moves the desktop window.

## Concepts
gameplay screen, HUD, system window, hp bar, exp bar, stat panel, battle log, damage popup, announcement, drag window

## Decisions
- Laid out for the 640x240 strip `MainConstants` docks the window as; the ScreenManager's CanvasScaler in MainScene is set to that reference resolution.
- The battle is a side-on stage drawn in UI: parallax `RawImage` layers scrolled by `uvRect`, and `SpriteActor` figures flipping pixel-art frames. Which creature looks how comes from `EnemyStateVO.Look`; the view only maps it to frames.
- A new creature (a new `SpawnId`) plays the old one's death, then the party walks while it slides in - the same time gameplay holds it in `ArrivalTurns`.
- The pixel art under `Art/` was generated by code (32x32 frames, point filtered), not drawn by an artist.
- All five heroes have a figure in the prefab; only those in `HeroStateVO.Members` stand on the stage, placed front to back by their order there. A figure already standing is never switched off and on, or its animation would restart every turn.
- The level-up choice is a panel inside this screen rather than a screen of its own: it is up while the battle runs on behind it. A click sends `UpgradeChosen`; the panel comes down on `HideUpgradeOffer`, which gameplay sends once the pick is applied - by the player or by the auto-pick.

## Known gaps
- The Bloodlines - Dark UI pack was not in the project, so the HUD is not skinned with it yet.
- The text uses the built-in legacy font, not a pixel font.
